Support (English)

Antikacı is an antique shop game: open crates, restore what you find, appraise it, haggle with customers and grow your shop. Questions or problems? Email merttgogdemirr@gmail.com.

Missing a purchase? Tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the in-game store. Your progress is stored only on your device, and Game Center is optional.
